Our Services

We provide support, education, fresh produce, and seeds to grow greener communities in Ilford.

What We Offer

Organic Farming

We cultivate fresh vegetables and herbs using eco-friendly, pesticide-free methods. Our produce is grown locally in Ilford, ensuring freshness and sustainability.

Community Workshops

Join our hands-on workshops to learn about composting, vertical gardening, zero-waste practices, and more. Perfect for beginners and experienced gardeners alike.

Weekly Produce Box

Subscribe to our seasonal produce box, filled with fresh, locally grown vegetables and herbs. Delivered straight to your doorstep every week.

School & Youth Programs

We partner with schools to teach children and young adults about sustainability, gardening, and the importance of growing their own food.

Seed Selling

We offer high-quality seeds for home gardening, including heirloom vegetables, aromatic herbs, and vibrant flowers. Perfect for starting your own garden.

Urban Garden Design

Transform your space into a thriving urban garden. We provide consultation and design services tailored to your needs, whether it’s a balcony, rooftop, or backyard.
